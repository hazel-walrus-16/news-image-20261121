One drawn picture that sums up the world news of Saturday 21 November 2026, in 28 reports: one lead, eight cards and nineteen briefs. The lead is Russia's biggest barrage of the war on Ukraine — 400+ drones and 110 missiles — with two missiles and a suspected drone coming down in Poland. Eight cards cover the AI sell-off dragging the S&P 500 into correction, the World Court's Myanmar genocide ruling, COP31 running past its deadline on fossil fuels, the FBI crime database hack, the daraxonrasib pancreatic-cancer trial, the 6.8-magnitude quake in the Peruvian Andes, Anthropic's claim that Claude solved the Riemann hypothesis, and Iran's call for diplomacy being rejected by Trump. A two-column briefs strip then rounds up nineteen more of the day's stories, from the Shenzhen trilateral summit and the UK–EU post-Brexit deals to Estonia closing its Russia border crossings, Kane's torn ankle ligament and Russell's pole position in Las Vegas.

The image is drawn by `draw.py` (Python + Pillow, measured layout, no photograph and no AI image generation), and `main.ts` hands the finished PNG to the grader byte for byte. The drawing is fully deterministic — no clock, no network, no randomness — so running `draw.py` twice produces identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 draw.py
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
